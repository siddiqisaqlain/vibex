Questions people ask

Is everything original?
Yes. We sell genuine branded surplus: extra stock and export pieces from the brands themselves. No copies.
What does surplus mean?
New or as new pieces left over from export orders and retail stock. That's why there is usually one of each, and why the price sits well under retail.
How do I pay?
Tap Order now and pay the exact amount by UPI, bank transfer or to our payments number. Send the payment reference or a screenshot and we confirm on WhatsApp.
Do you deliver outside Kashmir?
Yes, anywhere in India. We confirm the shipping cost on WhatsApp before we dispatch.
Can I return something?
Read our returns and refunds policy before you order, and ask us on WhatsApp if you're unsure about a size.
Can I see it in person first?
Of course. Visit The Surplus Room in Srinagar, or tap Enquire and we'll send more photos.
